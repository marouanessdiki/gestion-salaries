# 👤 Simple HR Profile - Practical Features

## 🎯 **What This Profile Provides**

A **simple, practical HR profile** designed for the real workflow of HR staff who handle in-person attestation requests.

---

## ✨ **Key Features**

### **📋 Personal Information Management**
- **Basic Details**: Name, email, phone, position
- **Department Info**: Shows "Ressources Humaines" 
- **Editable Fields**: Click "Modifier" to update information
- **Simple Avatar**: Shows initials of HR user

### **📊 Real-Time Statistics**
- **Total Employees**: Current number in database
- **Attestations This Month**: Generated this month
- **Total Attestations**: All-time count
- **Auto-Updates**: Refreshes when you visit the profile

### **⚙️ Simple Settings**
- **Auto-Save**: Automatically save form data
- **Show Stats**: Display statistics on dashboard
- **Easy Toggle**: Simple on/off switches

### **ℹ️ System Information**
- **Role**: Shows "Responsable RH"
- **Access Level**: "Gestion complète employés et attestations"
- **Last Login**: Shows connection status

---

## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **Access the Profile:**
1. Login as HR user
2. Click **"Mon Profil"** in the top navigation
3. View your information and statistics

### **Edit Information:**
1. Click **"Modifier"** button
2. Update any field you need
3. Click **"Sauvegarder"** or **"Annuler"**

### **Adjust Settings:**
1. Toggle **"Sauvegarde automatique"** on/off
2. Toggle **"Afficher les statistiques"** on/off
3. Changes save automatically
