## 🎨 **Design System**

### **🎯 Color Palette**
```css
Primary Blue: #1976d2 → #42a5f5 (NETCON gradient)
Success Green: #4caf50 (status indicators)
Warning Orange: #ed6c02 (statistics)
Neutral Grays: #f5f5f5 → #424242 (backgrounds)
Text Colors: High contrast ratios for accessibility
```

### **📐 Spacing & Layout**
- **Container**: 1200px max width with auto margins
- **Card Padding**: 32px for generous breathing room
- **Grid Spacing**: 32px gaps between major sections
- **Border Radius**: 16px for cards, 12px for inputs
- **Shadows**: Layered shadows for depth (8px, 12px, 16px)

### **✨ Animation System**
- **Fade Transitions**: 800ms, 1000ms, 1200ms staggered entrance
- **Hover Effects**: 0.3s cubic-bezier easing for smooth interactions
- **Transform Animations**: translateY(-8px) for card hovers
- **Icon Animations**: Scale and rotate effects on stat card icons
- **Button Feedback**: Lift and shadow effects on interaction

### **🖥️ Desktop (1200px+)**

- **Large Typography**: h2 (3.5rem) for main title

### **📱 Tablet (768px - 1199px)**
- **Adaptive Grid**: Statistics stack to 2x2, main content stacks

- **Medium Padding**: 24px card padding
- **Responsive Buttons**: Full width on smaller screens

### **📱 Mobile (< 768px)**
- **Single Column**: All elements stack vertically
- **Touch-Friendly**: Larger touch targets (48px minimum)
- **Readable Text**: Optimized font sizes for mobile
- **Compact Spacing**: Reduced padding for mobile efficiency
